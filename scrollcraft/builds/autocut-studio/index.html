Questions owners ask

Who is it for?

Agencies producing short vertical video ads for several brands at once. If your week is briefs, editors and client approvals, it was built for you.

Do we pay for AI on top?

Image and video generation runs on the Higgsfield plan your client already pays for, connected per brand, so there is no second AI bill for those. You see the estimated cost of a run before it starts.

Can clients review without a seat?

Your team decides who sees what. Reviews happen per scene, and an approval is always a person clicking a button, never a timeout.

How is it priced?

Per agency and per brand. We go through it with you when we set up your workspace.
