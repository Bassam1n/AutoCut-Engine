How agencies run on AutoCut

  1. 1

    Set up brands and your team

    One workspace per client brand: products, styles, rules and keys. Invite editors, assign them brands, and give clients review access without handing over the keys.

  2. 2

    Generate, then direct

    A brief becomes scripts, a storyboard and scene media. Your editors split scenes, rewrite lines and regenerate shots — the AI does the heavy lifting, your people make the calls.

  3. 3

    Review, approve, deliver

    Work moves across a board your whole team sees. Reviews happen per scene, revisions loop until it’s right, and an approved ad is ready to export and ship to the client.

Questions agencies ask us

Who is AutoCut Engine for?

Agencies and in-house teams producing video ads for multiple brands. If you juggle briefs, editors and client approvals every week, it was built for you. It’s not a consumer “make me a video” toy.

Do you charge for AI usage?

No. You connect your own provider keys (image, voice and video models), pay providers directly, and see the estimated cost of every generation before it runs. Spend limits pause a workspace automatically.

Can clients review work without a full seat?

Yes — reviews happen per scene inside the app, and your team controls who sees what. Approval is always a person clicking a button, never a timeout.

How do I get access?

Request access below. We set up your agency workspace, you get a sign-in link by email, and you can invite your team the same day.
